# Member quick start

Draft for the Scholarship Chair to review before invitations. This file does not send an email or edit the live FAQ.

1. Open the personal setup link in your invitation and choose a password. Then sign in and save your recovery key privately and confirm that you saved it. Do not share the link, password or recovery key.
2. Visit [the Scholarship Portal](https://scholarship-ato-kh.vercel.app/). Sign in using your email and password. A badge number or portal ID assigned by the Chair also works.
3. Open **My classes**. Add each class with its credit hours and a PDF or image of its syllabus. The Chair reviews both together; approved class hours add up to your account total. Wait for approval before submitting graded assignments for that class. Profile pictures and account settings remain in **My profile**.
4. Select **Submit an assignment** on an approved class, or **New submission** from the overview. Choose the activity from the standard dropdown; the optional search narrows its list without changing an existing selection. Enter its requested details and attach clear evidence. Enter your actual grade or hours; the form does not assume them. Check the information, then **Submit for review**. For several assignments in the same class, **Submit & add another** keeps the activity, class and date while clearing the assignment details and evidence.
5. Track the result in **My submissions**. Search by assignment or class and filter by status. Pending points are estimates; approved points count toward the calendar goals. Open a submission to read review notes and decision history. Ask the Chair to correct a mistaken decision; you do not need to submit the same activity again.
6. If you forget your password, use the password-reset option or your saved recovery key. If neither works, contact the Scholarship Chair. If the key screen was interrupted, sign in again and save the replacement key offered by the portal.

## FAQ drafts

The Chair can add or edit these individually under **FAQ → Add question**. Do not overwrite existing chapter answers without reviewing them.

**How do I get an account?**

The Scholarship Chair checks the chapter roster and sends you a personal setup link. Use it to choose your own password. Being on the roster does not automatically send an invitation.

**What can I use as my username?**

Use the email on your chapter account, your assigned portal ID, or a badge number the Chair has added to your account. New members can use their email or assigned portal ID before receiving a badge number.

**Why can't I select my class for an assignment?**

Add the class, its credit hours and its syllabus in **My classes**. The Scholarship Chair must approve the syllabus before you can submit a major, minor, lab or other graded assignment for that class. Replacing a syllabus starts a new review.

**Can I finish a submission later?**

**Keep draft & close** retains one unfinished submission in the current tab. Opening **New submission** restores it, including verified evidence. **Start over** clears the draft. Reloading, closing the tab, signing out or changing semesters clears it; drafts are not saved to your account or browser storage. A failed submission keeps your entries available to correct and retry.

**What should my evidence show?**

Follow the instructions shown for your selected activity. Make the relevant assignment, date, result or attendance easy to read. Upload only the information needed for the Chair to review that claim. Live uploads accept PDF, PNG and JPEG files up to 5 MB; profile pictures use images.

**How do my credit hours change?**

Enter hours when adding each class. Only Chair-approved class hours count toward your account total. Pending replacements or a denied replacement keep previously approved hours. The Chair can correct an approval or its hours; revoking an approved class removes its hours. Removing an approved class also needs Chair approval. If your account already has an older manually entered total, the first class-hours approval replaces it with the sum of approved classes.

**Why aren't pending points in my approved total?**

Pending points are estimates until the Chair reviews the submission. Only approved points count toward your goal. Denied submissions do not add points; open the submission to read the reason.

**Who can see my files and points?**

You can see your own records. The Scholarship Chair can review member submissions, syllabi and supporting files. Other members do not get access to your records.

**What should I do with my recovery key?**

Save the 16 words privately, preferably in a password manager, and confirm that you saved them. A replacement key makes the older key invalid. If a recovery attempt is interrupted, follow the on-screen message: wait two minutes before trying the new password, then the previous password, or use email password reset. Do not send the key to the Chair or put it in a submission.

**What happens at the end of the semester?**

The Chair can start a new semester and delete the previous semester's live academic records and uploaded files. Your account remains. You will add your new classes and syllabi for the new term. Backups and files people downloaded are governed by their separate retention policy.

**Does the test account save my work for the chapter?**

The public test sandbox uses temporary records in that browser tab. It does not submit work to the chapter database. Closing a tab normally ends that session, but browser session restoration can bring it back; use Reset demo to explicitly clear its records. Chair-created pilot accounts are different: they use the live database and are visible to the Chair.


## Chair shortcuts

- **Roster → Members**: search by name, email or sign-in ID. Use **Adjust points** for an audited manual balance adjustment, **Manage credits** for that member's classes, and **Account settings** for their name, badge or tier.
- **Review queue**: filter to Approved or Denied, open a submission and choose **Correct decision / points**. Enter the new award or denial and a reason. This replaces the previous award; the history keeps both decisions.
- **Classes**: requests are grouped by member. Select that member's pending classes, or choose individual classes, then **Review selected approvals** to approve up to 20 together. Check each syllabus and its hours first; confirmation includes selections hidden by a search or filter. A stale decision or invalid total saves none of the batch. Unsaved hours and notes survive filtering and other class saves while this screen stays open. Single approvals continue with the same member's next pending class.
- **Class corrections**: use Approved, Denied or All classes to find an earlier decision, then **Correct decision / hours**. Every correction needs a reason. Class-hour changes recalculate pending point estimates; previous assignment awards stay as recorded.
- **Roster → Invitations**: see accepted, awaiting and expired links. Select all not accepted or all expired, review the recipients, then send. Use **Correct email** to fix an address while preserving the member's records, then send a fresh invitation. Saving the address ends old portal access and sends no email. **Roster sheet** holds eligibility refresh and optional account preparation.
